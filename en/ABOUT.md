## Course Philosophy: Bogleheads

This course is based on the **Bogleheads** philosophy — an investment approach named after John Bogle, founder of Vanguard and creator of the first index fund.

### Core Principles

```
1. Costs matter
   └── Low expense ratios are critical for long-term returns

2. Diversification reduces risk
   └── Don't put all your eggs in one basket

3. Index funds beat most active managers
   └── 85-90% of active funds underperform the index over 15+ years

4. Time in the market beats timing the market
   └── Being invested matters more than picking the right moment

5. Simplicity is an advantage
   └── A portfolio of 2-4 ETFs is sufficient for most investors

6. Discipline and patience are key to success
   └── Stay the course
```

## Why This Approach

### Passive Investing Works

```
Research shows:

SPIVA Scorecard (S&P Global, 2023):
├── 87% of active funds underperformed S&P 500 over 15 years
├── 92% of active funds underperformed over 20 years
└── Reason: high fees + inability to consistently predict the market

Vanguard Research:
├── Each 1% in fees reduces capital by ~25% over 35 years
├── Low costs are the main predictor of fund success
└── Simple portfolios work as well as complex ones
```

### Why Not Active Trading

```
Statistics:
├── 70-90% of day traders lose money
├── Average retail investor lags the market by 1-3% per year
├── Reasons: commissions, taxes, emotional decisions
└── Attempts to "catch the bottom" lead to missing the best days

Example:
├── Miss 10 best days over 20 years → returns cut in half
├── Miss 20 best days → returns cut by 4x
└── Best days often follow immediately after worst days
```

## What You Need to Understand Before Starting

### Investing Is Risk

```
⚠️ IMPORTANT TO UNDERSTAND:

You can lose money:
├── S&P 500 dropped 57% in 2008-2009
├── S&P 500 dropped 34% in 33 days in March 2020
├── S&P 500 dropped 25% in 2022
├── Bonds also fall (2022: -13%)
└── No asset guarantees returns

Past results DO NOT guarantee future results:
├── Historical data is history, not a forecast
├── Market may not grow for 10-15 years ("lost decade")
└── Diversification reduces but doesn't eliminate risk
```

### This Is a Long-Term Strategy

```
Investment horizon:
├── Minimum: 5 years
├── Recommended: 10+ years
├── Ideal: 20-30 years
└── Short-term goals → not the stock market

Why long-term:
├── Volatility smooths out over time
├── Compound interest works on long horizons
├── More time to recover from downturns
└── Lower taxes (long-term rates are lower)
```
