# 📝 Practice and Exercises

Practical materials to reinforce your learning.

## What's Here

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Exercises** | Hands-on assignments for each module |
| **Checklists** | Ready-to-use lists for your actions |
| **Templates** | Planning and tracking spreadsheets |

## Getting Started

1. Complete at least the first 3 course modules
2. Work through the exercises for modules you've finished
3. Use checklists when opening your account and making trades
4. Fill out templates for your investment plan

> 💡 **Tip**: Practice is the key to success. Don't skip the exercises!
