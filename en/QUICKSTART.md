# Quick Start: Your First $500 in 7 Days

> **Goal:** Go from "thinking about investing" to "I have investments" in one week.

---

## Before You Begin

### Make Sure You're Ready

```
□ No credit card debt
□ Have emergency fund (at least 3 months, target 3-6)
□ Won't need this money for 5+ years
□ Prepared for temporary drops in value
```

If unsure — complete [Module 0](/investing-course/en/module-00-preparation/) first.

## Day 2-3: Open Your Account

### Documents Needed

**For US Residents:**
```
├── Social Security Number (SSN)
├── Driver's license or passport
├── Home address
└── Employer information
```

**For Non-US Residents:**
```
├── Passport
├── Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement)
├── Tax ID from your country
└── Sometimes: proof of income source
```

### Which Account Type?

```
Are you a US Resident?
│
├── YES → Do you have 401(k) with employer match?
│         │
│         ├── YES → 401(k) first (up to match), then Roth IRA
│         └── NO → Roth IRA (if eligible by income)
│                  or Taxable Account
│
└── NO → Taxable Account (regular brokerage account)
          + Complete W-8BEN form!
```

### Step-by-Step Process

```
Step 1: Fill in personal details
├── Name, address, date of birth
├── Citizenship and residency
└── Contact information

Step 2: Answer questions
├── Investment experience (can answer "Limited")
├── Financial situation
├── Investment goals
└── Risk tolerance

Step 3: Verify identity
├── Upload documents
├── Wait for verification (1-3 days)
└── Receive confirmation email

⚠️ IMPORTANT for non-residents:
Complete W-8BEN form immediately after opening account!
This reduces dividend tax from 30% to 10-15%.
```

### Common Registration Mistakes

```
❌ Typos in name → problems with documents
❌ Wrong address → important mail won't arrive
❌ Skipping W-8BEN → overpaying taxes
❌ Too aggressive risk profile → may be denied options trading
```

## Day 4: Fund Your Account

### Funding Methods

**For US Residents:**
```
ACH Transfer (recommended):
├── Free
├── 2-3 business days
└── Set up in bank or broker app

Wire Transfer:
├── Fast (same day)
├── May have fee ($15-25)
└── For urgent cases
```

**For Non-US Residents:**
```
International Wire Transfer:
├── SWIFT transfer from your bank
├── Bank fee (~$20-50)
├── Currency conversion to USD
├── 1-3 business days
└── Keep all receipts!
```

### How Much to Transfer?

```
Minimum to start: $100-500
├── Enough to buy ETF
├── Psychologically okay to lose temporarily
└── Can add more later

Recommended first transfer: $500-1,000
├── More flexibility
├── Lower relative fees
└── Good starting point

Don't transfer everything at once:
├── Keep your emergency fund!
├── Better: regular small transfers
└── DCA (dollar cost averaging) works well
```

## Day 5: Your First Purchase

### What to Buy?

**For absolute beginners — one ETF:**

```
VTI — Vanguard Total Stock Market ETF

Why VTI:
├── Entire US market (~3,500 companies)
├── Maximum diversification
├── Expense ratio: 0.03% ($3 per $10,000/year)
├── One of the world's largest ETFs
└── Couldn't be simpler
```

**Alternatives:**
```
VOO — Vanguard S&P 500 (500 largest companies)
SPY — SPDR S&P 500 (same thing, slightly higher fee)
ITOT — iShares Total Stock Market (similar to VTI)
```

### How to Buy — Step by Step

```
Step 1: Log into broker app/website

Step 2: Search for ETF
├── Type in search: VTI
├── Verify: "Vanguard Total Stock Market ETF"
└── Check current price

Step 3: Click "Trade" or "Buy"

Step 4: Fill out the order
├── Action: Buy
├── Quantity: enter dollar amount
│   (for example: $500)
├── Order Type: Limit (~0.1-0.2% above current price)
├── Duration: Day
└── Account: your account

Step 5: Review details
├── Approximate price
├── Number of shares
├── Commission ($0)
└── Total

Step 6: Confirm
├── Click "Submit" or "Place Order"
└── Done! 🎉

Step 7: Verify
├── Go to "Positions" or "Portfolio" section
├── VTI should appear in your holdings
└── Take a screenshot for your records!
```

### Market vs Limit Order

```
Limit Order (recommended for your first purchase):
├── Executes at your price or better
├── To buy almost immediately, set the limit slightly above
│   the current quote (~0.1-0.2%) — a "marketable limit"
├── Protects you from a bad fill on a sharp move
└── This is also what Module 8 recommends

Market Order:
├── Executes immediately at the current price
├── Only for liquid ETFs (VOO/VTI/SPY)
│   mid-day
└── Avoid at the open (9:30) and close (16:00)
```

## Day 6-7: Set Up Automation

### Automatic Investments

```
Why:
├── Removes emotions from the process
├── No need to remember
├── DCA concept works
└── "Pay yourself first"

How to set up:
1. Find "Automatic Investments" in broker app
2. Select ETF (VTI)
3. Enter amount ($100-500/month)
4. Choose date (e.g., 1st of month)
5. Link bank account
6. Activate
```

### DRIP — Dividend Reinvestment

```
What it does:
├── Dividends automatically buy more shares
├── Accelerates compound growth
├── No manual action needed
└── Usually enabled by default

How to check:
1. Settings → Dividends
2. Verify: "Reinvest dividends" = ON
```

### What's Next?

```
Week 2-4:
├── Complete the rest of the course modules
├── Don't touch your investments
├── Continue automatic purchases

After one month:
├── Check that automation is working
├── Look at balance (but not often!)
└── Increase contributions if you can

After one year:
├── Evaluate progress
├── Consider diversification (add international?)
└── Rebalance if needed
```

## Common Questions About First Purchase

**Q: What if the market drops tomorrow?**
```
A: That's normal and even good — your next purchases
   will be at lower prices. You're investing for 10+
   years. Short-term moves don't matter.
```

**Q: Is $500 too little?**
```
A: No! The important thing is to start. $500/month
   at 10% annually for 30 years becomes ~$1.13M.

   ⚠️ This is not a guarantee. 10% is the long-run nominal
   historical US return; the real (after-inflation) return is
   lower (~6–7%), and forward-looking estimates for the next
   10 years are around 4–6% real. Treat this as a rough guide,
   not a promise.
```

**Q: Why VTI specifically?**
```
A: Maximum simplicity + diversification + low costs.
   For a first purchase — perfect.
   You can add complexity later.
```

**Q: When should I add other ETFs?**
```
A: When you've completed the entire course and
   understand why. Many investors hold only VTI
   their whole lives and do great.
```
