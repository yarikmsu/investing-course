### VT vs VTI + VXUS

```
Two approaches to global diversification:

Approach 1: One ETF
└── VT (100%)

├── VTI (60%) — US
└── VXUS (40%) — World ex-US

├── Simplicity (one ETF)
├── Automatic rebalancing
└── Fewer transactions

VTI + VXUS Pros:

├── Tax-loss harvesting between them
└── Slightly lower total expense ratio (0.04% vs 0.07%)

For most people, VT is an excellent choice!
```

### Why Do You Need BND?

```
Bonds ≠ Returns
Bonds = Stability and ballast

Role of BND in a portfolio:
├── Reduces overall volatility
├── Source of funds for rebalancing
├── Protection during stock market drops
└── Stable income (coupons)

Example 2022:
├── S&P 500 (VOO): -18%
└── BND: -13%

Example 2008:
├── S&P 500: -37%
└── Bonds: +5%

Bonds cushion the falls!
```

### Impact of Interest Rates

```
⚠️ IMPORTANT TO UNDERSTAND:

Rates ↑ = Bond prices ↓
Rates ↓ = Bond prices ↑

BND Duration = 6.2 years

This means:
├── Rates +1% → BND drops ~6%
└── Rates -1% → BND rises ~6%

At high rates (like 2024):
├── Current yield is high (~4.5%)
├── When Fed cuts rates, BND price will rise
└── Good time to buy bonds

At low rates:
├── Current yield is low
├── Risk of drop when rates rise
└── Less attractive
```

### Who BND Is For

```
✅ SUITABLE FOR:
├── As part of a diversified portfolio
├── To reduce volatility
├── Investors closer to retirement
├── Conservative investors
└── For rebalancing (sell bonds, buy stocks on dips)

❌ NOT SUITABLE FOR:
├── Young aggressive investors (0% bonds OK)
├── Those seeking maximum growth
├── As the only investment
└── With 20+ year horizon (stocks alone may be better)
```

## Which ETF to Choose?

### For Beginning Investors

```
Simplest choice:

If you believe in the US:
└── VTI (or VOO) — 100%

If you want the whole world:
└── VT — 100%

If you want balance:
└── VTI (80%) + BND (20%)

Start with one ETF. You can always add complexity later.
```

### By Risk Profile

```
Aggressive (25-35 years):
├── VTI: 70%
├── QQQ: 20%
└── VXUS: 10%

Moderate (35-50 years):
├── VTI: 60%
├── VXUS: 20%
└── BND: 20%

Conservative (50+ years):
├── VTI: 40%
├── VXUS: 10%
└── BND: 50%

Ultra-simple (any age):
└── VT: 100%
```

### Key Takeaways

```
1. VOO/VTI — excellent choice for most people
2. QQQ — only as PART of portfolio (high risk)
3. VT — for "one ETF for everything" fans
4. BND — to reduce volatility
5. Expense Ratio matters over the long term

Most important:
├── Start investing
├── Hold long-term (10+ years)
├── Don't panic during drops
└── Contribute regularly (DCA)
```
