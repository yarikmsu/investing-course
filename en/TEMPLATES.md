## How to Use Templates

```
OPTION 1: Paper
├── Print the templates you need
├── Fill in by hand
└── Store in a folder

OPTION 2: Digital (recommended)
├── Copy to Google Docs / Notion / Obsidian
├── Fill in and update
└── Store in cloud

OPTION 3: Spreadsheets
├── Transfer to Google Sheets / Excel
├── Add formulas for automatic calculations
└── Create progress charts
```
