# Module 0: Are You Ready to Invest?

> **IMPORTANT: This is educational material, not financial advice.**
>
> **Investing involves risks. You can lose some or ALL of your invested money.**
>
> - S&P 500 dropped 57% (2008-2009), 34% (2020), 25% (2022)
> - Past results DO NOT guarantee future performance
> - Consult with a licensed financial professional (CFA/CFP) before investing

> **Complete this module before starting to invest.** It will help ensure you're financially and psychologically ready.

## Lesson 0.1: Financial Health

### Where Are You Now?

Before investing, answer these questions honestly:

```
□ I know my monthly income
□ I know my monthly expenses
□ I have no credit card debt
□ I have savings for unexpected expenses
□ I won't need this money for the next 3-5 years
```

**If you can't check at least 3 of these items — solve these problems first.**

### The 50/30/20 Rule

A simple system for distributing income:

```

         ▼
```

## Lesson 0.2: Debt — The Investor's Enemy

### Good and Bad Debt

| Category | Debt Type | Interest Rate | Action |
|----------|-----------|---------------|--------|
| ⚠️ **Bad** | Credit cards | 15-25% | Pay off first! |
| ⚠️ **Bad** | Personal loans | 10-20% | Pay off first! |
| ⚠️ **Bad** | Payday loans | 100%+ | Pay off immediately! |
| ⚠️ **Bad** | Auto loan (expensive car) | 8-15% | Pay off or sell |
| 🟡 **Neutral** | Student loans | 5-8% | Pay on schedule |
| 🟡 **Neutral** | Auto loan (affordable car) | 5-10% | Pay on schedule |
| ✅ **Acceptable** | Mortgage | 3-7% | Can invest in parallel |
| ✅ **Acceptable** | Business loan | 5-10% | If profitable |

### The Math: Debt vs Investments

```
Situation:
├── Credit card debt: $5,000 at 20% APR
├── Available money: $5,000
└── Expected investment return: 10%

Option A: Invest
├── After one year: $5,500 in investments
├── Debt grew to: $6,000
└── Net result: -$500

Option B: Pay off debt
├── Debt: $0
├── Saved on interest: $1,000
└── Net result: +$1,000

Conclusion: Paying off 20% debt = a guaranteed
           20% return. Investments have historically
           returned ~10% on average (nominal, before
           inflation) and are NOT guaranteed.
```

> ℹ️ That's exactly the point of the contrast: paying down debt is a **certain** return (you save precisely the interest you'd otherwise pay), while equity returns are **uncertain** and vary year to year. That's why high-rate debt is worth clearing first.

### Debt Payoff Strategies

**Snowball Method:**
```
1. Pay minimums on all debts
2. Put all extra money toward the SMALLEST debt
3. When it's paid off — move to the next
4. Psychological wins motivate you to continue
```

**Avalanche Method:**
```
1. Pay minimums on all debts
2. Put all extra money toward the HIGHEST RATE debt
3. Mathematically optimal method
4. You save more on interest
```

### When Can You Invest With Debt?

```
✓ CAN invest if:
├── Only debt is a low-rate mortgage
├── Employer offers 401(k) match
│   (free money — don't miss it!)
└── Debt interest rate < 6-7%

✗ CANNOT invest if:
├── Have credit card debt
├── Debt interest rate > 10%
└── No emergency fund
```

## Lesson 0.3: Emergency Fund

### Why Do You Need One?

```
Without emergency fund:
├── Lost your job
├── Need money urgently
├── Have to sell investments
├── Market just dropped 30%
└── Lost $3,000 of $10,000 😢

With emergency fund:
├── Lost your job
├── Live on emergency fund for 3-6 months
├── Don't touch investments
├── Market recovers
└── Everything is fine 😊
```

### How Much Do You Need?

```
MINIMUM: 3 months of expenses
├── For stable employment
├── For dual-income households
└── If easy to find a new job

OPTIMAL: 6 months of expenses
├── For most people
├── For single-income households
└── Standard recommendation

MAXIMUM: 12 months of expenses
├── For freelancers
├── For unstable income
├── For those who sleep better with more
└── For pre-retirement age
```

### Where to Keep Your Emergency Fund?

```
✓ SUITABLE:
├── High-Yield Savings Account (HYSA) — 4-5% APY
├── Money Market Account
├── Short-term Treasury Bills
└── Savings account at a reliable bank

✗ NOT SUITABLE:
├── Stock investments (volatility!)
├── Cryptocurrency (even worse!)
├── Cash at home (inflation + risk)
└── Regular checking account (0% APY)
```

### How to Build Your Emergency Fund?

```
Step 1: Calculate your goal
├── Monthly expenses: $3,000
├── Goal (6 months): $18,000
└── Already have: $2,000 → Need: $16,000

Step 2: Automate
├── Set up auto-transfer on payday
├── Start with $200-500 per month
└── Increase when possible

Step 3: Accelerate
├── Bonuses → emergency fund
├── Tax refund → emergency fund
├── Side gig → emergency fund
└── Goal: 12-18 months to full emergency fund
```

## Lesson 0.4: Investment Readiness Test

### Take the Test

Answer honestly. Score 1 point for each "Yes".

```
FINANCIAL READINESS:

1. I have no credit card debt
   □ Yes  □ No

2. I have an emergency fund (at least 3 months of expenses)
   □ Yes  □ No

3. I know my monthly income and expenses
   □ Yes  □ No

4. I have stable income
   □ Yes  □ No

5. I won't need this money for the next 5 years
   □ Yes  □ No

PSYCHOLOGICAL READINESS:

6. I understand investments can temporarily drop 30-50%
   □ Yes  □ No

7. I'm ready to not touch investments for at least 5-10 years
   □ Yes  □ No

8. I won't panic and sell during market drops
   □ Yes  □ No

KNOWLEDGE:

9. I understand the difference between stocks, bonds, and ETFs
   □ Yes  □ No

10. I understand that no one can guarantee returns
    □ Yes  □ No
```

### Test Results

```
9-10 points: ✅ YOU'RE READY!
You can start investing.
Proceed to the Quick Start guide.

7-8 points: ⚠️ ALMOST READY
Complete the full course before starting.
Pay attention to items where you answered "No".

5-6 points: ⏸️ WAIT
First solve your financial issues.
Create an emergency fund.
Pay off high-interest debt.

0-4 points: 🛑 NOT READY
Investing now will cause more harm than good.
Focus on your financial foundation.
Return in 6-12 months.
```

## What If You're Not Ready?

### 6-Month Plan

```
Month 1-2: Sort out your budget
├── Track all expenses
├── Find where to cut back
└── Determine amount for savings

Month 2-4: Pay off bad debt
├── List all debts
├── Choose a strategy (snowball / avalanche)
└── Direct all extra money to payoff

Month 3-6: Build emergency fund
├── Open a HYSA
├── Set up automatic transfers
└── Save at least 3 months of expenses

After that: Return to investing!
```
