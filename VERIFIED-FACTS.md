## Notes for editors

- The **enhanced 60–63 catch-up replaces** the regular age-50 catch-up (it is not stacked on top).
- The **NRA estate-tax section is the single highest-value addition** — it is a real wealth-loss risk for the non-resident audience and the primary reason to use Irish UCITS ETFs.
- When stating SALT, never write a flat "$40,000 through 2029" — use $40,000 (2025) / $40,400 (2026) with the escalation + 2030 reversion note.
